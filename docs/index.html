Use it in HubSpot

This page is for testing a profile. To score leads automatically in HubSpot, build the agent from the spec: download the HubSpot agent spec and paste it into Agent Hub, Agent Builder.

How scoring works

A lead is a bad fit right away if its industry is excluded, its email is a free mail address, or its title has a bad word. Otherwise it earns points: industry 35, company size 25, job title 25, country 15. 70 or more is a good fit, 40 to 69 is maybe, under 40 is bad. The message always names what is missing or off.
